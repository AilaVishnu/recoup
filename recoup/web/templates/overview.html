Rates are value-weighted: a merchant cares what share of rupees came back, not what share of tickets did, and with this amount distribution the two are not close.

This differs from the figure scripts/run_eval.py prints, and should.

That report weights by event, so every recovery counts once regardless of size; this page weights by rupee. With 70% of at-risk value sitting in 12% of events the two diverge sharply, and neither is wrong - they answer different questions. The report also derives lift from per-event attribution against the frozen counterfactual, which a real deployment could not do; the arm difference shown here is what a live merchant could actually measure.
